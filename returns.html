Store Policies

Returns & Shipping

Last updated: 12 October 2026

1. Shipping Rates & Delivery Times

We process physical store orders (such as our 2-Disc CDs) carefully. Delivery estimates and rates are split by region:

  • United Kingdom: Free Shipping (Estimated 2-5 business days)
  • Europe: £8.99 flat rate (Estimated 3-7 business days)
  • Rest of the World: £13.99 flat rate (Estimated 5-10 business days)

Order processing and handling time takes 1-2 business days before dispatch. You are responsible for any applicable local customs duties or import taxes outside of the UK.

2. Cancellations, Returns & Refunds

Your statutory consumer rights are fully respected. You have the right to cancel and return your physical-goods purchase within 14 days of receiving your items.

  • Condition: To be eligible for a return, items (such as physical CDs) must be unopened, unused, and in their original packaging where applicable.
  • Return Postage: Unless an item arrived faulty or incorrect, you are responsible for paying your own return shipping costs.
  • Processing Refunds: Once we receive and inspect your returned item, your refund will be processed and automatically applied to your original payment method via Stripe within 5-10 business days.

3. Damaged or Faulty Items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, please reach out to us immediately via our contact form with your order reference number and photos of the issue. We will arrange a replacement or issue a full refund including shipping costs.
