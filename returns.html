Store Policies

Returns & Shipping

Last updated: 14 October 2026

1. Shipping Rates & Delivery Times

We process physical store orders (such as our 2-Disc CDs) carefully. Delivery estimates and rates are split by region:

  • United Kingdom: Free Shipping (Estimated 2–5 business days)
  • Europe: £8.99 flat rate (Estimated 3–7 business days)
  • Rest of the World: £13.99 flat rate (Estimated 5–10 business days)

Order processing and handling time takes 1–2 business days before dispatch. You are responsible for any applicable local customs duties or import taxes outside of the UK.

2. Returns, Refunds & Conditions

We accept returns for both defective and non-defective products within 14 days of delivery.

  • Product Condition: Items must be in new, unused condition.
  • Return Method: Returns are handled by mail using a downloadable, free print-at-home return label provided upon request[cite: 12]. There are no restocking fees[cite: 12].
  • Exchanges: We do not offer direct item exchanges; customers wishing for a different item should place a new order and return the original[cite: 12].
  • Refund Processing: Once your return is received and inspected, approved refunds are processed back to your original payment method via Stripe within 5 business days[cite: 12].

3. Damaged or Defective Items

If your physical order arrives damaged or defective, please contact us immediately via our contact form with your order reference and photos. We will evaluate the issue and ensure you are fully taken care of.
