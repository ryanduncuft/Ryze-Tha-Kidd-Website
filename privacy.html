Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

1. Who We Are

Ryze Tha Kidd operates this website and its official store. This policy explains how we collect, use, store and share personal information when you browse the site, contact us, join a mailing list, or buy merchandise, physical music or digital downloads.

For privacy questions or to make a request about your information, please use the contact form and title your message “Privacy request”.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the site, we may collect:

  • contact details, such as your name, email address and the contents of messages you send us;
  • order details, including the products purchased, order reference, delivery status and purchase history;
  • shipping and billing details needed to fulfil a physical order;
  • newsletter preferences and records of consent or unsubscribe requests; and
  • technical and usage information, such as IP address, browser/device information, pages viewed and cookie identifiers.

We do not receive or store full payment-card numbers. Payment information is entered directly with our payment provider.

3. How We Use Information

We use personal information to operate the website, process and deliver orders, provide download access, respond to enquiries, prevent fraud or misuse, maintain records, meet legal and tax obligations, and send marketing only where you have opted in or where otherwise permitted by law. We rely on contract performance for purchases, legitimate interests for site security and service improvement, legal obligations for required records, and consent where it is required for marketing or non-essential cookies.

4. Payments, Hosting and Service Providers

Payments are processed by Stripe. Stripe processes payment and fraud-prevention information under its own privacy notice; we receive the transaction information needed to manage the order. We use Cloudflare services to deliver and secure shared site assets, which may process limited technical information such as IP addresses and request logs. We may also use carefully selected fulfilment, email, analytics, hosting and support providers where needed to run the service. These providers may process information only for their relevant service and subject to their own applicable terms and privacy notices.

5. Cookies and Analytics

We use essential cookies and local storage to make the site work, remember display and performance settings, and protect the service. If analytics, advertising, or other non-essential cookies are enabled, we will request consent where required and explain the relevant choices at that time. You can also control cookies through your browser settings; blocking some cookies may affect site features.

6. Retention and Security

We keep information only for as long as needed for the purposes above, including to fulfil an order, resolve a dispute, prevent fraud, and meet accounting or legal obligations. Retention periods vary by record type. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or portability of your personal information, and you may object to certain processing. You may withdraw marketing consent at any time by using the unsubscribe link in a marketing email or by contacting us. We may need to verify your identity before responding. You may also complain to the data-protection authority that applies where you live; in the UK, this is the Information Commissioner’s Office.

8. International Processing

Our providers may process information outside your country. Where required, we take steps intended to ensure that an appropriate transfer mechanism or safeguard applies.

9. Children

The store is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate permission. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our practices, services or legal obligations change. The current version will be posted on this page with its updated date.
